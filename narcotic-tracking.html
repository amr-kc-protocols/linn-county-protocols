Training videos for field personnel

Linn County EMS is moving narcotic tracking into Operative IQ. Controlled substances are counted, signed for, administered and restocked in the Frontline app instead of on paper.

Each short video below covers one task. If you are new to the system, start with the full walkthrough, then use the task videos as refreshers when you hit that step on shift.

⚠ These videos need a signal. The rest of this app works offline, but the videos stream from YouTube. Watch them at the station or on cell service — not somewhere you are counting on offline access.
